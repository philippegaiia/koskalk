# Data

## Separate alkali solution from dilution liquid
Keep internal lye_liquid identifiers stable, but never expose “lye liquid” or literal equivalents. The completed NaOH/KOH mixture is the alkali solution; water, milk, hydrosol, and substitutes are dilution liquids. Apply this distinction consistently to headings, costing labels, controls, accessibility copy, and validation.
