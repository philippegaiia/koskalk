# Schemas

## Ingredient editor uses task-oriented top-level tabs
Keep the admin ingredient editor organized with top-level task tabs: General, Market declarations, Guidance & media, Translations, Soap chemistry, Allergens, Restricted substances, IFRA, and Components. Show canonical INCI once; market declarations are explicit overrides, with the EU override hidden unless it differs from canonical INCI. Do not expose market effective-date fields in this form.
