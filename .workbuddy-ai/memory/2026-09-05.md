## Evaluated a journey-based revision brief on the audit → all 6 corrections hold

Owner pasted a review brief proposing the audit be reorganised around three journeys (customize a
platform ingredient / duplicate / create) with six corrections, and asked me to **evaluate** the
comments rather than implement. Verified each against code; recorded as §7 of the audit doc.

**All six are correct:**
- **F1** `isReadOnly()` really is `private` (`IngredientEditor.php:1380`), so my §4 #1 was
  unimplementable — Blade can't call it. `soapChemistryAvailable()` too (:1389).
- **F5** classification already handled: `->visible(fn (Get $get) => $get('ingredient_structure') === 'blend')`
  (:749). I framed designed behaviour as a bug.
- **F2** I over-applied `views.md`'s WordPress rule — it covers *public marketing / editorial /
  long-form docs*, not in-app ingredient domain data. Withdrawn (§7.1 + inline in §6.7).
- **F3** cards and Save bar ARE mutually exclusive: `blade.php:40` `@if ($isPlatformIngredient)` …
  `:180` `@endif` encloses both cards; Save bar is in `@unless` at `:185`. My "three save models" was
  wrong. Withdrawn inline.
- **F6/F11** the existing dirty pattern is bigger than the indicator I proposed reusing — it has
  `dirtyStateRegistry.blocksNavigation()` (component.js:1109) and a `beforeunload` listener (:1138).
  My "indicator with or without autosave" would have thrown away the protection.
- **F8** `duplicateSourceData()` (:545) writes `user_authoring.trusted_koh_sap_value` +
  `trusted_fatty_acid_profile`; `hasInheritedSoapChemistry()` reads them. Duplication is a flow, not
  just a warning remedy.

**Three refinements the brief missed:** F2 survives F3's death (`{{ $this->form }}` renders
unconditionally at :182-184); F5's real defect is that `->visible()` reads *live form state* while
`persistTabInQueryString` (:646) keeps a stale tab in the URL; and duplication has hard blockers —
**a lipid with no `sapProfile->koh_sap_value` cannot be duplicated at all**
(`duplicate_soap_profile_required`, :200), soapmaking alkalis are platform-only, and images are
dropped.

**One pushback:** don't just widen `isReadOnly()` to public. It conflates platform-read-only (form
inert BUT two editable cards) with Viewer-read-only (everything inert). Journeys need **two** signals
— `isPlatformReference()` and `canEditIngredient()` — or Viewers and platform-customisers render
identically, which is the exact inconsistency the brief ranks first.

Revision itself NOT done — §4 still not reorganised, no acceptance criteria written.

## §4 rewritten around three journeys (analysis-only, doc now 878 lines)

Owner said proceed, with refinements that improved on my pushback: **don't prescribe a number of
booleans — reuse the existing capability checks.** Agreed, and that is what §4.1 now does:

- **Origin** (`owner_type === null`) drives *layout*.
- **Capability** drives *controls*, via the three methods that **already exist and are already
  public**: `Ingredient::isEditableBy()` (Ingredient.php:392), `canEditWorkspaceGuidance()`
  (:522), `canEditWorkspaceMaterialCode()` (:1343).
- Only two Blade gates change. `:185` `@unless ($isPlatformIngredient)` should gate on the
  ingredient-data capability instead of origin — that alone removes the reachable 403.
- `isReadOnly()` (:1380, **private**) is the thing to stop using: it folds origin and capability
  together, so it can't express "form read-only BUT two cards below it editable", which is exactly
  journey A.

Structure: §4.1 permission model · §4.2 customize · §4.3 duplicate · §4.4 create · §4.5 Viewers ·
§4.6 Composition · §4.7 unsaved changes · §4.8 priorities · §4.9 unverified. Each journey has
Current / Recommended / **acceptance criteria**.

**not** establish it is desirable, and added to §5 as an open question.
