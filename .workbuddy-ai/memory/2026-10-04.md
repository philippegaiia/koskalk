## UI: page ground too green on some displays + section shadows too big (asked for advice, then applied)

Owner report: the light green background is "perfect on my Mac but too much on some displays", and the
section shadows are "a bit too big". Both traced to single tokens in `resources/css/shared/soapkraft.css`.

- **Green = `--color-surface`, the page ground** (`<body data-user-shell class="… bg-[var(--color-surface)]">`,
  `layouts/app-shell.blade.php:33`; also `print.blade.php` and `welcome.blade.php`). Not the section tone
  band. Was `oklch(96.4% 0.016 128)` global / `oklch(97.2% 0.010 128)` in the user shell — hue 128 is
  green. Two real causes: **perceived saturation grows with area** (the ground is the biggest painted
  area in the app), and **near-neutral tints sit inside the spread of panel white points**, so a
  calibrated wide-gamut Mac cancels them while a cheaper panel adds to the hue. Gamut is *not* it —
  C 0.016 at L 96% is well inside sRGB.
  Fix: chroma roughly halved, lightness +0.3 so it stays paper not grey → **0.009 / 96.7%** global,
  **0.0055 / 97.5%** user shell. Small surfaces (`--color-active-soft`, `--color-success-soft`, the 22
  badge hues) deliberately keep their chroma — small areas need more of it to register.
- **Shadow = `--shadow-card`** (`0 12px 24px rgba(60,50,30,.07)` → **`0 6px 14px -4px rgba(60,50,30,.06)`**,
  contact layer unchanged). ~half the footprint, negative spread keeps it hugging the edge. Read by
  `.sk-card`, `.sk-ingredient-info-popover`, `.sk-nav-rail` and the `shadow-card` utility. Left alone on
  purpose: `.sk-card-elevation` (public homepage, a deliberate step up), `.sk-card-elevation-subtle`, and
  the select `::picker` popover (0 12px 24px / .12) — those float above content and need the throw.

**Two contract tests pin `--color-surface` by exact string** — `RecipeWorkbenchDesignPolishTest.php:2219`
(global) and `:2312` (user shell). Both updated in the same change; 176 passed, Pint PASS. Recorded as a
rule in `.ai/rules/css.md` (writes to the main checkout — the tree was already on `main`).

## Evaluation only: `#F7FDFB` section ground + the three blue section headers

Owner asked me to evaluate (not change) two things. **No code touched in this round.**

- **`#F7FDFB` is not in the codebase.** Not a literal anywhere (no hex, no Tailwind teal/emerald), and a
  script that resolves every `oklch()` token *and* every `color-mix(in oklab, …)` in
  `resources/css/**/*.css` produces nothing closer than `#FAFAF6` (`.sk-inset`, Δ6.6). The nearest real
  surfaces are `--color-reference-header` `#F5F9FD` (blue) and the warm neutrals (`panel #FDFBF7`,
  `section-head #FCFAF6`). `#F7FDFB` is mint (G>B>R); nothing in the palette is mint — so it is most
  likely a screen/colour-picker reading, i.e. the same display-drift phenomenon as the earlier
  green-ground complaint.
- **Contrast is not the problem.** Measured (pipeline validated against the known anchor
  `ink-soft #5e5851` on `panel #fcfaf6` = **6.690**, matches exactly): `#F7FDFB` gives ink **13.26**,
  ink-soft **6.78**, ink-muted **5.36** — better than the card panel (13.19 / 6.75 / 5.33) and within
  3% of pure white. So "damping contrast" is perceptual (warm ink on cool ground loses edge
  definition), not a WCAG issue.
- **Owner clarified the hex is the browser's own oklch→hex conversion** (browser shows
  `oklch(0.x …)`, copies as hex). Back-converting settles it: **`#F7FDFB` → `oklch(0.989 0.0068 174)`**
  — same lightness and chroma as `--color-panel` `oklch(0.988 0.006 85)` (`#FDFBF7`), hue ~90° off. At
  C 0.006 the a/b components are ~0.0005/0.006, so a rounding-level offset in the readback swings the
  hue number wildly while the rendered colour is visually identical. **Nothing mint exists in the
  stylesheet; the sample is the near-white panel read back with a slight cool cast.**
- **The blue is real and is `--color-reference-header: oklch(98.0% 0.007 245)` = `#F5F9FD`.** Exactly
  three users: `ingredient-browser`, `formula-analysis`, `fatty-acid-profile` (all
  `sk-section-header-reference`), plus `.sk-combobox-reference` selection/autofill. Critique: hue 245 is
  a **third hue family** — the palette is warm (50–95) plus botanical green (145–166); 245 is
  `--color-info`'s hue (230), so the header silently claims "informational/materials" semantics those
  panels don't have. And at C 0.007 it reads as cold grey, not as a deliberate blue — the same
  almost-neutral-tint trap as the green ground. Recommendation: keep `--color-reference-heading`
  (`#35414C` slate ink) and `--color-reference-line`, drop the blue ground to the warm section tint.
