  `RecipeWorkbenchPayloadNormalizer.php:156,330`, `SubstanceComplianceService.php:121`,
  `RecipeWorkbenchVersionPayloadMapper.php:90`. **`InciGenerationService::declarationRuleState()`
  does not use it** — it has its own `Str::lower(trim(...))` + `'eu'` at `:172-173`, so fixing the
  model alone would not propagate.
- `FormulaSharePresenter::settings()` `:46` filters to `is_string || is_int || is_float` — **a boolean
  is silently dropped**. Any `screening_enabled` flag added there vanishes with no error. Carry it
  outside `settings()`, don't widen the filter.
- The workbench is Livewire + Alpine, **not Filament**; `Repeater` exists only in `app/Filament/**`
  and `IngredientEditor.php`. Don't prescribe Filament components for workbench settings.
- `SubstanceComplianceService` is a **generic regime-driven substance engine**, not "the EU 2023/1545
  engine"; its rules are placeholder `watch` rows from `SubstanceSeeder.php:44-58` whose own
  `source_reference` says to replace them. EU 2023/1545 is the *allergen* side (`InciGenerationService`).
- `label_market` does **not** exist today — only a translation key (`workbench.php:399`). The existing
  "Label market" switcher *is* the regime switcher (`label-market-switcher.blade.php:10` binds
  `regulatoryRegime`) and is asserted by `RecipeWorkbenchDesignPolishTest.php:1617-1619` and
  `CanadaLabelMarketTest.php:163`.
- IFRA "current certificate" is soft: `is_current` boolean, no uniqueness, no effective-date
  resolution; `IngredientDataEntryService::syncIfra()` deletes and recreates limits.

  and seven test files depend on it; its `fragrance` phase reuses `$liquid`, which is **not**
  `requires_aromatic_compliance`, so IFRA assertions against it would read zero load (false green).
